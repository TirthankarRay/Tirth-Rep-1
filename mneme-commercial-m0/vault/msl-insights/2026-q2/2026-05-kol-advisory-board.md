# KOL advisory board — May 2026 readout

## Context

Half-day advisory board with eight KOLs in Boston. Topics: front-line use,
post-progression strategy, and the role of liquid biopsy in surveillance.

## Key Quotes / Observations

- Six of eight panelists endorsed front-line BrandX where MET-exon14 is the
  driving alteration.
- Unanimous interest in a head-to-head trial, with clear awareness that this
  is unlikely from the sponsors.
- One KOL raised a question about acquired-resistance mechanisms; the BrandX
  team is preparing a translational-research summary in response.

## Implications

- Medical-affairs publication plan should prioritize a real-world durability
  comparison versus historical BrandY cohorts.
- IIT pipeline should include at least one post-progression sequencing study.
