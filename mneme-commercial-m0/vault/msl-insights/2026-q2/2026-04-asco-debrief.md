# MSL field intelligence — ASCO 2026 debrief

## Context

The MSL team attended ASCO 2026 with 14 field MSLs in coverage rotation. This
note captures recurring scientific questions and competitive intelligence.

## Key Quotes / Observations

- **Durability narrative landed**: most KOLs spontaneously referenced the BrandX
  longer median PFS during informal hallway conversations.
- **Liquid biopsy preference is consolidating**: 8 of 12 advisory board members
  said liquid biopsy is now their default first-line MET testing modality.
- **Concern about hepatic AEs**: 3 KOLs asked specifically about transaminase
  monitoring protocols. MSLs delivered the approved monitoring guide.

## Implications

- Reinforce monitoring guidance in upcoming MSL touchpoints.
- Liquid-biopsy preference accelerates the case for the diagnostic-partner pull-through.
- Track the next-gen MET asset (CompetitorCo) — early signal of strategic intent.
