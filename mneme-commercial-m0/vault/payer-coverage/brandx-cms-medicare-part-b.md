# BrandX coverage — CMS Medicare Part B

## Current Coverage

BrandX is covered under Medicare Part B for biomarker-positive patients per the
April 2026 NCD update. No prior authorization. Reimbursement is at ASP + 6%.

## Recent Policy Changes

The April 2026 NCD update explicitly included BrandX following the FDA approval.
LCDs in all MAC jurisdictions are aligned.

## Field Implications

Part B coverage is the cleanest of any payer; Part B-skewed accounts (academic
centers, infusion-heavy community practices) are highest-yield commercial targets.
