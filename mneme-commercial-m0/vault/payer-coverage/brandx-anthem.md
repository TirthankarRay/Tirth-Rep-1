# BrandX coverage — Anthem commercial

## Current Coverage

Anthem commercial places BrandX at tier 3 with prior authorization and a step-edit
through BrandY. The step-edit is the primary barrier and adds an average of 14 days
to time-to-fill in Anthem-heavy markets.

## Recent Policy Changes

Anthem reaffirmed the BrandY step-edit in its March 2026 policy update. The
market-access team is preparing a formal request for parity placement supported
by the durability narrative once the BrandX final label is published.

## Field Implications

Set expectation with prescribers that Anthem patients will require a BrandY trial
unless documented contraindication. Patient-support program staff should proactively
prepare appeals templates.
