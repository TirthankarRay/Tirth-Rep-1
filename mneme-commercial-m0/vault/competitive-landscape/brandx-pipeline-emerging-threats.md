# Emerging competitive threats to BrandX, 2026-2028

## Near-Term (2026 H2)

CompetitorCo (BrandY sponsor) presented a late-breaker at ASCO 2026 outlining a
next-generation MET inhibitor (development code NEXT-MET-22) targeting BrandY
non-responders and acquired-resistance settings. The asset is in Phase II with
projected accelerated-approval timing in late 2027.

If approved on that timeline, NEXT-MET-22 would erode BrandX's switch-after-BrandY
opportunity. The countermove is to entrench BrandX as the first-line option through
guideline updates and academic-pathway placement.

## Medium-Term (2027–2028)

ChallengerInc has disclosed a combination program pairing BrandZ's EGFR backbone
with a MET inhibitor, indicated for EGFR-mutant patients with MET-mediated resistance.
This is a small population (estimated ~6% of EGFR-mutant relapses) but represents a
defensible niche the BrandX team should monitor.

## Watchlist

- Two undisclosed bispecific MET/EGFR programs in IND-enabling work (one Asia-based)
- The biosimilar question is not relevant for BrandX in this horizon (small molecule,
  novel composition-of-matter through 2034)
- Diagnostic-side: a next-generation liquid-biopsy MET assay from the testing partner
  is expected H1 2027 and will materially affect test-rate KPIs (positive)
