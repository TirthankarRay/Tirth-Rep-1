# BrandX vs BrandZ — adjacent EGFR landscape

## Positioning vs Competitor

BrandZ is an EGFR T790M-targeted therapy and does not directly compete with BrandX
in the MET-exon14 segment. However, the two assets compete for share of the same
oncologist's prescribing attention, particularly in community settings where the
practitioner sees a broad mix of driver mutations.

The BrandZ field team has a strong "test everyone for EGFR" narrative, which
indirectly benefits BrandX: the same upfront NGS panel that identifies an EGFR
T790M patient also identifies MET-exon14. Co-detection rates in audited test
panels show ~3% of EGFR-tested patients also have actionable MET alterations.

## Clinical Profile Comparison

The two assets do not have a head-to-head trial and cannot be promoted as such.
BrandZ's target population overlaps BrandX's in less than 2% of advanced NSCLC
patients (those with both EGFR T790M and MET co-amplification — these are typically
treated with EGFR-first regimens).

## Commercial Implications

- **Field collaboration angle**: encourage panel testing rather than single-gene
  workup. This benefits both brands and is the right clinical practice.
- **No direct payer overlap**: parity placement is the norm and is not contested.
- **Co-promotion potential**: consider with the diagnostic partner; not with BrandZ
  directly (separate companies).
