# BrandX launch narrative & message map

## Core Narrative

The MET-exon14 subset of NSCLC has historically been underdiagnosed and undertreated.
BrandX changes that equation by pairing a clear biomarker-driven indication with a
clinically meaningful durability profile. Our narrative places BrandX as the catalyst
for systemic, test-first practice change — not just a new molecule.

## Pillar Messages

- **Pillar A — Identify the patient.** "If you're not testing for MET-exon14, you're
  missing patients who can benefit from a targeted therapy."
- **Pillar B — Match the patient to the therapy.** Cite the biomarker-selected ORR
  and median PFS from the registration trial.
- **Pillar C — Stay on therapy.** Reinforce the tolerability profile and the
  patient-support program (financial, adherence, AE management).

## Message Discipline

All claims must be MLR-approved. Pillar B messaging may only be delivered using
the approved visual aid set, version 2026.1.
