# BrandX overall commercial strategy, 2026

## Positioning

BrandX is positioned as the targeted-therapy option of choice for biomarker-selected
advanced NSCLC patients, with a clinical narrative anchored on durable response in the
MET-exon14 subpopulation. The 2026 commercial frame is "the right patient, the right
test, the right line" — emphasizing partnership with academic centers on biomarker
testing as a prerequisite for prescribing.

## 2026 Plays

1. **Test-and-treat lift.** Drive a 25% YoY increase in MET-exon14 test rates in
   community oncology centers through co-promotion with the diagnostic partner.
2. **First-line conversion.** Shift treatment patterns by quantifying the durability
   advantage in front-line use, supported by the new Phase IIIb subgroup analysis.
3. **Academic anchor.** Maintain preferred-pathway status in at least 70% of
   designated NCI comprehensive cancer centers.

## KPIs

- New patient starts: +18% YoY
- MET-exon14 test rate in community oncology: 38% (baseline 30%)
- Preferred-pathway placement: 28 of 41 NCI sites
- Brand share within MET-exon14 NSCLC: ≥55%
