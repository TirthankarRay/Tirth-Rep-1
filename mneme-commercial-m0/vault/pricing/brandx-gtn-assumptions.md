# BrandX gross-to-net assumptions, 2026

## Price

Blended GTN assumption: **41%** (gross-to-net deduction as % of WAC).

Channel-weighted breakdown:
- Commercial: 38%
- Medicare Part B: 22%
- Medicaid: 65%
- 340B: 78%

## Rationale

Assumptions modeled on the BrandY observed GTN trajectory with adjustment for the
parity-coverage scenario. Outcomes-based contracts are assumed at 6% of commercial
volume in year 1.
