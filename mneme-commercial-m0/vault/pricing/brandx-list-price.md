# BrandX US list price

## Price

Wholesale Acquisition Cost (WAC) per 30-day course: **$23,400**.
This corresponds to roughly $280,800 annualized.

## Rationale

Set at parity with the BrandY launch WAC and within 8% of the indication-class
average for second-line oral MET-targeted therapies. The rationale memo from the
pricing committee weighs commercial competitiveness against the access strategy
in Medicare Part B accounts.
