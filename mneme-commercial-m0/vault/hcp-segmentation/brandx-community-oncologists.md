# BrandX — community oncologist segment

## Segment Definition

Community oncology practices, including large networks (US Oncology, Florida Cancer
Specialists, etc.) and independent groups. Represents the majority of NSCLC patient
volume nationally.

## Engagement Strategy

- **Biomarker testing**: the primary call-to-action; lift test rate from 30% → 38%.
- **Sales rep coverage**: tier-1 and tier-2 prescribers in person; tier-3 via digital.
- **Co-promotion**: partnered with the diagnostic company for combined testing pulls.
