# BrandX — academic medical center segment

## Segment Definition

NCI comprehensive cancer centers and large academic systems. Approximately 41 sites
nationally. High influence on guideline development; concentrated prescriber volume
in early-line, biomarker-tested patients.

## Engagement Strategy

- **Pathway placement**: target preferred-pathway in 28+ sites (current: 22).
- **Investigator-initiated research**: support 4–6 IIT proposals from designated sites.
- **MSL frequency**: monthly tier-1 cadence, with quarterly thought-leader summits.
